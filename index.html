              Keep your music playing

              Your music keeps playing even with the screen off. But Android's battery saver can pause it in the background. Turn it off for your browser (or for Metal Radio once it's installed as an app):

              1. Open your phone's Settings
              2. Tap Apps, then find your browser
              3. Tap Battery
              4. Choose Unrestricted
